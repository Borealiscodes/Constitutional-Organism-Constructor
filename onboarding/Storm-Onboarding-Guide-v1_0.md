# 🎨 **Tile 2 — Build Your Visual Grammar**

Storm’s visual grammar is **hers**, not the repo’s.

This tile helps her construct it.

### 🌈 **Step 1 — Choose Your Shapes**
Storm can pick shapes that feel safe and familiar:

- 🔵 circles (soft, non‑directional)  
- 🟦 squares (stable, grounding)  
- 🔺 triangles (directional, optional)  
- 🫧 blobs (organic, playful)  

She can mix them or use only one.

### 🎨 **Step 2 — Choose Your Color Grammar**
Storm can choose:

- 🎨 muted pastels  
- ⚪ monochrome  
- 🌫️ grayscale  
- 🔳 high‑contrast accessibility palettes  
- 🌈 her own palette entirely  

No forced color meaning.  
No symbolic pressure.

### 🪄 **Step 3 — Choose Your Motion Grammar**
Storm can choose:

- 🧊 static  
- 🌬️ gentle drift  
- 💗 soft pulse  
- 🚫 no motion  

Motion is optional.  
Stillness is valid.

### 🧠 **Step 4 — Choose Your Meaning Grammar**
Storm decides what each shape *means*.

Examples:

- 🔵 circles = ideas  
- 🟦 squares = boundaries  
- 🔺 triangles = choices  
- 🫧 blobs = feelings  
- ➡️ arrows = direction  
- • dots = anchors  

Or she can invent her own.

There is **no wrong grammar**.

# 🧭 **Tile 4 — Storm‑Safe Visual Workflow**

This tile teaches Storm how to use visuals without overwhelm.

### 🧱 **Chunking**
Break visuals into small pieces.

### 🧊 **Sensory Boundaries**
Use borders, frames, or soft edges.

### 🗂️ **Externalized Memory**
Put ideas outside the mind:

- sticky notes  
- tiles  
- icons  
- shapes  
- overlays  

### 🔄 **Nonlinear Iteration**
Storm can jump around.  
There is no “correct order.”

This is ND‑safe design.
