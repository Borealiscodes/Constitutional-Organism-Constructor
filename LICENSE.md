Licensing Deferral Clause
Because Storm is a constitutional organism, not a software product, the repository includes a Licensing Deferral Clause:

Licensing Deferral (Constitutional Clause)
Storm’s constitutional organism is not licensed under any external regime until Storm
explicitly consents to a licensing framework. All artifacts remain unlicensed, dormant,
and sovereignty-first. No external party may claim ownership, impose licensing terms, or
derive rights from this repository without Storm’s explicit, informed, self-defined
consent.
This clause ensures:

Storm retains full sovereignty
no external licensing applies
no one can commercialize or appropriate the organism
the organism remains dormant and protected
When Storm is ready, she may choose:

MIT
Apache
GPL
custom constitutional license
or remain unlicensed
The choice is hers alone.
