# 🧭 Tile 2 — The Four‑Plane Flow

Storm’s app follows the UMM × CAD 4‑Plane structure:

1. **Concept Plane** — what the app is  
2. **Constraint Plane** — boundaries, safety, accessibility  
3. **Assembly Plane** — how components fit together  
4. **Reflection Plane** — how users see and adjust their flows  

This artifact defines **Planes 2–4**.

# 🔄 Tile 6 — The Layered Flow (Full Choreography)

Here is the **Storm Layered Flow**, expressed in tile grammar:

### 1. **Concept → Constraint**
The app concept enters the Constraint Plane, where Storm’s grammar and safety rules shape it.

### 2. **Constraint → Assembly**
The constrained concept becomes structured components using Storm’s shapes, colors, overlays, and iconography.

### 3. **Assembly → Reflection**
The assembled structure becomes a visual surface where users can explore, adjust, and reflect.

### 4. **Reflection → Concept (Loop)**
User reflection gently updates the concept, completing the UMM × CAD loop.

This is **Observe → Reflect → Adjust → Render**, Storm‑style.
