### 🌩️ Storm App Concept Plane v1.0  
*Concept Layer · Sovereignty‑First · ND‑Safe · Visually Accessible · Dormant*

---

# 🧩 Tile 1 — What This Plane Is

This is the **Concept Plane** for Storm’s app.

It defines:

- what the app *is for*  
- who it is meant to help  
- how it should feel  
- what boundaries it must respect  

It is:

- a **top‑layer map**, not a spec  
- a **concept surface**, not a feature list  
- a **Storm‑paced orientation**, not a roadmap  

Everything here is **dormant** and **consent‑gated**.

---

# 🎯 Tile 2 — Core Intent

Storm’s app exists to:

- **help people reduce overwhelm**  
- **offer gentle, visually accessible structure**  
- **support ND‑safe workflows**  
- **externalize memory and ideas**  
- **make complex things feel navigable and kind**  

It is:

- supportive, not corrective  
- stabilizing, not demanding  
- playful, not trivializing  
- accessible, not cluttered  

---

# 💛 Tile 3 — Who It Helps

The app is for people who:

- get overwhelmed by complex systems  
- need visual scaffolding to think clearly  
- benefit from chunking and externalized memory  
- prefer gentle pacing and non‑linear exploration  
- need sensory boundaries and low‑spike visuals  

It is especially aligned with:

- ND‑safe design principles  
- visual thinkers  
- people who need “maps, not manuals”  

---

# 🎨 Tile 4 — How It Should Feel

The app should feel:

- **soft** — no harsh edges, no sharp spikes  
- **playful** — overlays, icons, gentle visuals  
- **safe** — no forced interpretation, no pressure  
- **clear** — simple planes, simple flows  
- **kind** — it meets people where they are  

Visual tone:

- pastel or low‑stimulus palettes  
- simple shapes (circles, squares, blobs)  
- optional overlays (stars, clouds, doodles)  
- minimal motion, unless explicitly chosen  

---

# 🧭 Tile 5 — Conceptual Structure (Planes)

The app concept follows a **4‑Plane workflow**:

1. **Concept Plane** — what the app is and why it exists  
2. **Constraint Plane** — boundaries, safety rules, accessibility constraints  
3. **Assembly Plane** — how components fit together  
4. **Reflection Plane** — how users see, adjust, and reflect on their flows  

This artifact defines **Plane 1**.

---

# 🧱 Tile 6 — Core Capabilities (Concept Level)

At the concept level, the app:

- provides **visual maps** of ideas, tasks, or systems  
- lets users **externalize memory** into tiles, nodes, or shapes  
- supports **chunking** and **layered views**  
- offers **gentle overlays** for mood, emphasis, or clarity  
- keeps everything **visually accessible** and **ND‑safe**  

No implementation is implied here.  
This is **what**, not **how**.

---

# 🔒 Tile 7 — Boundaries & Non‑Goals

The app is **not**:

- a diagnostic tool  
- a medical device  
- a cognitive model  
- a surveillance system  
- a productivity whip  
- a replacement for human support  

It must:

- never pressure users to “optimize”  
- never punish slowness or non‑linearity  
- never force a single “correct” way to think  

It is a **support surface**, not a judgment surface.
